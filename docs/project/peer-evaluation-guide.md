# Week 12 peer evaluation preparation

The course form is confidential and is handed in individually to the instructor/TA. Each member should rate every team member, including themselves, from 1 (very weak) to 5 (excellent) for code & tests, sprint commitment, teamwork, helping others, and overall. Include factual, concise evidence when answering who contributed most or whether anyone contributed much less than expected. Do not place completed confidential evaluations in the shared repository.

Before submission, each person should confirm the form is handed in. This guide is not a substitute for the official form.
